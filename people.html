Anesthesiology / Pain Medicine

Attending Physician

Dr. Ariana Nelson

Anesthesiology / Pain Medicine

UC Irvine

A board-certified anesthesiologist and interventional pain medicine specialist, Dr. Ariana M. Nelson serves as Associate Professor in the Department of Anesthesiology and Perioperative Care at UC Irvine School of Medicine. Dr. Nelson earned her MD at the University of Michigan Medical School, completed her anesthesiology residency at Northwestern Memorial Hospital, and remained at Northwestern for her fellowship in Multidisciplinary Pain Management. Her clinical practice focuses on radiofrequency ablation, peripheral nerve stimulation, and spinal cord stimulation for back, neck, joint, and neuropathic pain, while her research through the COMPREHEND Lab spans objective pain biomarkers, cannabis-related analgesia, AI-driven health literacy tools, and aerospace medicine in collaboration with NASA. She is a Dean's Scholar at UC Irvine School of Medicine, core faculty for the PRIME-LC program, and Program Co-Director of the Space MED Program, and she serves as Associate Editor of Anesthesia & Analgesia and Assistant Editor of Pain Medicine.

ariana.nelson@comprehendlab.com

Attending Physician

Dr. John Scolaro

Orthopaedic Surgery

UC Irvine

Dr. John A. Scolaro is an Associate Clinical Professor in the Department of Orthopaedic Surgery at the UC Irvine School of Medicine and a fellowship-trained orthopaedic traumatologist who treats acute fractures, nonunions, and malunions, with particular focus on pelvic and acetabular injuries, periprosthetic fractures, and complex periarticular fractures. He earned his MD at Boston University School of Medicine, completed his general surgery internship and orthopaedic surgery residency at the University of Pennsylvania, and finished his orthopaedic trauma fellowship at Harborview Medical Center in Seattle. Dr. Scolaro's research centers on patient-focused clinical outcomes, including multicenter trials and large population-based outcome measures, along with biomechanical work on fracture fixation conducted through the Orthopaedic Biomechanics Laboratory at VA Long Beach. He has been recognized with the Orange County Medical Association Physicians of Excellence award and serves as a mentor to UC Irvine medical students, including those pursuing artificial intelligence research in orthopaedic surgery.

john.scolaro@comprehendlab.com
Attending Physician

Dr. Jonah Hebert-Davies

Orthopaedic Surgery

UC Irvine

Dr. Jonah Hebert-Davies is an Associate Professor of Orthopaedic Surgery at UC Irvine School of Medicine, specializing in orthopaedic trauma and shoulder and elbow surgery, with clinical emphasis on upper extremity trauma and reconstruction, peri-articular fractures, non-unions, and shoulder and elbow joint replacement. He earned his medical degree and completed his orthopaedic surgery residency at the University of Montreal, followed by an orthopaedic trauma fellowship at Harborview Medical Center in Seattle and a shoulder and elbow surgery fellowship at Washington University in St. Louis. Dr. Hebert-Davies maintains active longitudinal research in shoulder and elbow fractures and arthroplasty outcomes, contributing publications, textbook chapters, and service on scientific and safety boards for multicenter studies. He serves as elbow section editor for the Journal of Orthopedic Trauma and is a recipient of multiple teaching awards including the 2025 Madigan Orthopedics Residency Teaching Award.

Attending Physician

Dr. Hao-Hua Wu

Orthopaedic Surgery

UC Irvine

Dr. Hao-Hua Wu is an Assistant Professor of Clinical Orthopaedic Surgery at the UC Irvine School of Medicine, where he specializes in the surgical management of cervical, thoracic, and lumbar spine disorders, including cervical laminoplasty, motion-sparing cervical disc replacement, tubular decompression for disc herniations, and robotic spine surgery. Dr. Wu completed his orthopaedic surgery residency at the University of California, San Francisco, followed by the Harvard Combined Spine Fellowship at Massachusetts General Hospital and Brigham and Women's Hospital. His research centers on patient-reported outcome measures in spine surgery, global orthopaedic health initiatives with surgical colleagues in Malawi and Tanzania, and leadership and medical education. Dr. Wu is lead author of the Gunner Goggles board exam preparation series and has delivered international lectures on conflict resolution and emotional intelligence for medical professionals.

Attending Physician

Dr. Sohaib Hashmi

Orthopaedic Surgery

UC Irvine

Dr. Sohaib Z. Hashmi is a board-certified orthopaedic spine surgeon at UCI Health who treats cervical, thoracic, and lumbosacral spine disorders, including degenerative disease, scoliosis, spinal stenosis, spinal infections, traumatic spine injuries, and complex revision and reconstructive surgery using both open and minimally invasive techniques. He earned his medical degree at the University of Toledo College of Medicine, completed orthopaedic surgery residency at Northwestern University Feinberg School of Medicine, an orthopaedic spine surgery fellowship at the University of Maryland Medical Center, and additional orthopaedic trauma fellowship training at Cedars-Sinai Medical Center. Dr. Hashmi serves as Director of Spine Trauma at UCI Health and holds appointments as Assistant Professor in the Departments of Orthopaedic Surgery and Neurological Surgery at the UC Irvine School of Medicine.

Attending Physician

Dr. Don Park

Orthopaedic Surgery

UC Irvine

Dr. Don Y. Park is a Clinical Professor of Orthopaedic Surgery at the UC Irvine School of Medicine, where he specializes in endoscopic, minimally invasive, and outpatient spine surgery. Dr. Park earned his medical degree from UC San Diego School of Medicine and completed both his orthopaedic surgery residency and spine surgery fellowship at Stanford University, joining UCI Health from UCLA, where he served as associate clinical professor and vice chair of quality and safety. His clinical and research interests center on biportal endoscopic spine surgery, endoscopic lumbar fusion, disc replacement, and motion preservation, areas in which he is internationally recognized as a key opinion leader and frequent invited lecturer. He serves as founding Director of the Advanced Endoscopic and Outpatient Spine Program at the UCI Health Comprehensive Spine Center, leading clinical education and program development in advanced endoscopic techniques.

Neurosurgery

Attending Physician

Dr. Michael Oh

Neurosurgery

UC Irvine

Dr. Michael Y. Oh is a board-certified neurosurgeon at UC Irvine specializing in complex spine surgery and stereotactic and functional neurosurgery, with clinical focus on endoscopic spine surgery, spinal cord stimulation, deep brain stimulation for movement disorders such as Parkinson's disease, spine tumors, and spinal deformity. Dr. Oh completed medical school at the Keck School of Medicine at USC, followed by a surgical internship and neurosurgery residency at Allegheny General Hospital in Pittsburgh where he served as chief resident, and a fellowship in stereotactic and functional neurosurgery at the University of Toronto. His research interests center on robotics for neurosurgical procedures, perioperative optimization of spine surgery patients, AI implementation for surgical outcomes, and augmented and virtual reality models for patient education, and he holds a patent for a device for the minimally invasive removal of intracerebral blood clots. He serves as Professor and Vice Chair of Education in the Department of Neurological Surgery, Director of the Comprehensive Spine Center, and Medical Director of Ambulatory Neurological Surgery Clinics.

michael.oh@comprehendlab.com

Fox is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Ophthalmology and Visual Sciences at the UC Irvine School of Medicine and Gavin Herbert Eye Institute, where he specializes in glaucoma and cataract surgery, including minimally invasive glaucoma surgery, laser therapy, uveitis, and home monitoring of intraocular pressure. PhD

Arshia Ilaty

PhD Student

UC Irvine

Arshia Ilaty is a Ph.D. student in Computer/Computational Science at UC Irvine, specializing in privacy-preserving machine learning and synthetic data generation for healthcare applications. With a strong foundation in both academic research and industry experience, Arshia has developed innovative solutions across healthcare analytics, blockchain technology, and autonomous systems at organizations including Tesla, World Mobile, and Turtle Beach. His research integrates generative AI, deep learning, and domain-specific biosignal processing to address critical challenges in personalized medicine, clinical trial retention, mental health monitoring, and early disease detection across diverse conditions including stress, pain, breast cancer, and metabolic disorders. Beyond research, Arshia is an active entrepreneur who secured funding from ZIP Launchpad for an AI-powered pet health monitoring system, demonstrating his ability to translate technical expertise into real-world impact. He serves as a reviewer for top-tier conferences, including NeurIPS, Chase, and IEEE CogMI, and actively contributes to open-source projects in machine learning. Driven by a passion for solving complex technical challenges, Arshia combines rigorous scientific thinking with practical engineering to advance the intersection of AI and healthcare.
